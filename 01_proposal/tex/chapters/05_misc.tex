\section{Why This Proposal Has Become Obsolete}
\label{ch:decogs}
Two days before turning this proposal in, I ran another search for papers dealing with my proposed idea. As I had so far not found one paper dealing with the approach of using frame deltas, I had been skeptical if my searches had been thorough enough. And on September 22nd I found a preprint called \textit{DecoGS} \cite{sulo2026decogsadaptivestaticdynamicdecoupling} which had only been uploaded seven days before. After reading, it became very clear that the authors had the very same ideas, pretty much down to the very details. 

They successfully implemented the proposed ideas and their evaluation shows that image quality is superior to previous solutions like 3DGStream. Training times could however not be reduced. As an accompanying repository has not yet been made available to the public, we can only guess as to why this is.

I am 43, from Berlin and I studied Diplom-Informatik at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, graduating in 2010. The idea of the thesis topic stems from an older thought experiment of mine, which saw true 3D video broadcasts of live events become possible.\\
I attended the seminar \enquote{Artificial Intelligence - Visual Computing} in the 2026 summer term, actually proposing 3DGS as a seminar topic early on. The seminar paper I then wrote on 3DGS forms an important knowledge base of this proposal and the proposed master thesis.
